Safety and alignment, mapped

Eleven field maps in this org are about making AI systems behave: what the field is, how a model is given values, whose instructions it should follow, what it may do with what it knows, how to test it, and what it represents inside. They were built separately and now link into each other. This page says what question each answers, suggests an order, and shows how they connect. Back to all maps.

A reading order

  1. Get the lay of the land: the capability versus alignment bridge, then AI Safety, then Technical AI Safety for the course-length version.
  2. Constitutions and model specs, as one stop: Constitutional AI for the 2022 paper and how it grew into Claude's 2026 constitution; the constitution ladder for that document in depth; OpenAI's Model Spec and its delta with Claude's constitution; then Instruction hierarchy for whose instructions count, which bridges back to the constitution.
  3. What an agent may do with information: Agentic privacy, then CIMemories for the memory case.
  4. Evaluations and red teaming, how to test all of it: HarmBench for the benchmark, then the red-teaming ramp-up for how the field does it.
  5. Interpretability, looking inside: the Pain Axis, for what a model represents about harm to itself and what steering that direction does.
