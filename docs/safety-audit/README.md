# Safety maps audit, 2026-11-22

The safety and alignment hub ([safety.html](../../safety.html)) links twelve field maps plus three bridge pages, about 260 pages in all. This audit looked for the same thing explained twice, stale facts, broken links, and which pages lack explanation modes, definitions or anything to poke. Detail per cluster: [constitutions](constitutions.md) (Constitutional AI, Claude's constitution, Model Spec, instruction hierarchy), [overview](overview.md) (AI Safety, Technical AI Safety, HarmBench, red-teaming ramp-up, Pain Axis, hub bridges), [privacy](privacy.md) (agentic privacy, CIMemories, the five glossaries).

## What we found

| Area | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Deploys | Four maps (AI Safety, agentic privacy, CIMemories, red-teaming ramp-up) were served from a side branch that had drifted from `main`. The live CIMemories site was missing its ICLR re-key, the CAPS bridge and the composition explorer, which broke five links from agentic privacy. | Each side branch merged into `main`; Pages now serves `main` everywhere. |
| Duplicates | Little copy-paste across maps (top section overlap 34%). Eight real "same explanation twice" cases, all inside or next to one map: the HarmBench copyright slice (harm page vs type page), the CoastRunners boat story (spec gaming vs reward hacking), Sleeper Agents retold on deceptive alignment, what a safety framework is (TAS 3.2 vs 3.3), the stale three-lab framework list on AI Safety's responsible-scaling page, "refuse everything scores 0%" repeated across HarmBench, the 2023-vs-2026 length argument (Constitutional AI bridge vs constitution p02), the four-case table (constitution p05 vs p15), plus an identical copy of the CAPS bridge in agentic privacy and CIMemories. | Keep one canonical explanation and cut the other to a sentence and a link. |
| Same topic, different depth | The 8 Constitutional AI chapters on the 2026 constitution vs the 18-part ladder; RLHF, interpretability, frameworks, HarmBench and the attack papers across AI Safety, TAS, HarmBench and the ramp-up. | Keep both, add the missing links (Constitutional AI never linked the ladder; nothing linked the Model Spec map; TAS and the ramp-up leaf pages linked no other map). |
| Stale facts | Instruction-hierarchy pages pinned to the 2026-08-18 Model Spec; "5 authority levels" vs six; AI Safety still describes the retired ASL ladder; old institute and framework names; HarmBench "three" vs "four" discrepancies and Table 6 labelled 510 instead of 400; AutoDAN called white-box and black-box; 45 vs 46 contexts; "violation" vs "gap" for a flow no norm covers. | Fixed on the pages, or labelled with the version they describe. |
| Links | No broken internal or cross-map links or anchors (about 3,000 checked, anchors included). | None needed. |
| Modes and definitions | Only LLM Judges had Plain / Technical / ELI5 term boxes. Five maps kept their own glossary pages, with about 18 shared terms defined differently. | The judges system becomes one shared script (`assets/terms.js`) and one shared glossary (`assets/glossary/`), used by every map. Words that mean different things on different maps (corrigible, system, guideline, black box, norm, reward hacking) get one entry naming both senses. |
| Interactives | Constitution explorables (six), the Constitutional AI pipeline, the instruction-hierarchy ladder and CIMemories explorers existed; the Model Spec, HarmBench, TAS, AI Safety, Pain Axis and hub pages had none. | New widgets built from numbers already on the pages (see the cluster files, section 4). |
